Who We Are

An institutional bridge between two nations.

SFC is a Singapore-domiciled company with multinational holdings across a diverse portfolio, including island and reclamation development, investment consortiums, global capital allocation, financial management, healthcare holdings, industrial fisheries and port operations, infrastructure development, medical services, global trading and commodities, mining and resources, and agricultural commodities — spanning twelve registered entities across Singapore, Indonesia, and the United States.

Our strength lies in vertical integration — from licensed sand concessions and a dedicated dredging fleet to the trading desk, from the fishery port to the investment portfolio and real estate — spanning five core divisions: fishery industrial ports, large-scale infrastructure and reclamation, a global trading house, real estate and resources, and strategic fund investment. Every venture is governed with the diligence, transparency, and long-term perspective that international partners and governments expect.

Our Journey
1988 — Present

Main supplier to Singapore reclamation

For over 38 years the Group has been a principal supplier of marine sand for Singapore's land-reclamation programme.

Early Years

Supply-only origins

In the early years the Group focused purely on sand supply rather than construction works, delivering directly to Korean and Japanese main contractors.

Riau Province

Indonesia's Riau Islands

All early volumes were sourced from concessions in Indonesia's Riau Islands, secured under the Chairman's appointment as Economic Advisor and Trade Commissioner.

Expansion

Regional expansion

Sourcing later extended to Vietnam, Cambodia, and the Philippines to sustain long-term supply to Singapore projects.

Pan-United, a Singapore-listed company, tendered for reclamation projects using the Group's licences, with the last Singapore tender concluded in 2019.
